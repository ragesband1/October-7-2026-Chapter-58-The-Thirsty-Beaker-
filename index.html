📜 The Story

Once upon a time there lived a forest. There was a bird named "The Thirsty Beaker" — proud, determined to find water all on its own, refusing help from anyone. After eating, it grew thirsty, but water could not be found. "I can do this alone!" it said. "I don't need anyone!"


Days passed. Pride kept it from asking. The next morning — the other birds found it too late. The Thirsty Beaker was gone.


From that day on, the forest birds lived differently — sharing, caring, helping. They learned: no one survives alone.

✨ Moral Lesson: Do not think only of yourself — that is pride. Before it's too late, care for others. Good karma comes to those who give care first. Be humble; think of others, and they will think of you. Caring for others is how they will care for you.
